## What the author contributes

Rental-domain modeling, stage-specific knowledge organization, task-skill design, operational document practice, private adaptation of a desktop assistant, and the selected case studies and engineering decisions in this repository.

The broader inventory includes work instructions and knowledge structures, not just software. Inventory categories such as test cases or decision records are not claims that every possible asset type has already been built or measured.

## Upstream and external capabilities

- [Jev](https://github.com/jev-chat/jev-chat-jarvis-mac) supplies the upstream desktop-assistant foundation. Its project is MIT-licensed; the license and notice accompanying the private copy are retained with the private archive. Upstream code, screenshots and benchmark results are not republished here as original work.
- [Ollama](https://docs.ollama.com/api/introduction) supplies the model-serving layer. The configured model is an external dependency, not a model trained by this project.
- [Notion](https://developers.notion.com/guides/mcp/get-started-with-mcp) and Codex supply their respective workspace and task-execution capabilities.
- Raycast is discussed as an alternative entry point. Unresolved candidate-agent names do not imply affiliation or integration.

## What remains private

Client records, contracts, invoices, complete operating procedures, phrases, skill instructions and references, knowledge snapshots, domain implementation and patches, business-specific rules and source-location records.

Public examples are newly constructed synthetic situations. They do not use redacted client screenshots. No claim is made that publishing a copyright notice prevents copying; confidentiality is maintained by retaining the underlying assets outside the repository.
