# Seasonal maintenance

[Project](../README.md) · [Changelog](../CHANGELOG.md)

Maintenance follows the rhythm of the business. The aim is a small, repeatable habit that keeps the operating source current.

| Period | Operating pattern | Review emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| January–April | Main rental season | Briefly capture recurring friction during work |
| After the main season | Consolidation and next-cycle preparation | Check Notion stages and repair frequent phrases; prepare public lists if outreach needs them |
| September | Smaller cycle, mainly overseas student applications | Address issues actually seen in overseas screening and move-in coordination |
| Other months | Routine operation | Correct individual issues when necessary |

## One maintenance path

**Observe an issue → verify it → update Notion → maintain the relevant phrase → update dependent skills or snapshots if needed → replay the scenario → record the revision.**

Notion is the first maintenance location. Update the relevant explanation, cautions and example there. A frequent, stable action can then become a callable phrase with a clear trigger, use sequence and expected result. The full wording and detailed operational rules stay private.

Routine notes need only identify the stage, problem, resolution and affected knowledge. They do not need an entire copied conversation. “Frequent” initially reflects operational judgment; no automated usage-frequency measurement is claimed.

## From learning to acquisition

When the business needs outreach, selected property or living-information lists can become public material for Xiaohongshu or other Chinese media. This is an optional output of the season, not a commitment to publish every year and not an export of the private operating library.

The connection is practical: client questions expose communication gaps; revised explanations improve future service; selected broadly useful information can introduce the service to future clients.

## What a public revision records

A dated entry states the problem category, what kind of behavior changed, and how the relevant scenario was checked. It omits client details, exact business rules and complete phrases. A revision affecting retrieval also notes whether its private snapshot was refreshed.

The first public record is 2026-10-21. This maintenance method is being documented for future use; no sequence of completed past seasonal reviews is implied.
